## Monthly client cycle

Every client follows the same 8 steps each month, so the work repeats instead of being reinvented. Day numbers are a guide to adjust.

1. **Day 1, intake (you + Coordinator):** collect goals, tone, offers, opening hours and photos in one short form.
2. **Days 1-3, audit and calendar (Strategy):** audit the Instagram and Google Maps pages, then draft the month's content calendar. You approve it.
3. **Days 3-5, text and briefs (Copywriting, Design brief, QA review):** captions, story texts and one brief per visual, checked before they reach you.
4. **Days 5-20, production (creative and devs):** the creative makes visuals and videos; devs handle site updates and the care checklist.
5. **Weekly, client approval (you):** send the week's posts to the client and get a written yes in the chat before publishing.
6. **Weekly, publishing (human):** schedule posts with a free tool such as Meta Business Suite. A person presses publish, not an agent.
7. **Weekly, ads check (Ads, Growth clients only):** review results and propose changes. The client pays the ad budget directly.
8. **Day 28, report and renewal (Reporting, Admin):** draft the report, send it with a 30-minute call, and issue the next invoice.

## Hard limits

Agents prepare and a human decides. These rules protect your accounts, your money and your reputation.

- **No automated sending.** Agents never send DMs, WhatsApp messages or comments on their own. Platforms restrict this and can ban the account.
- **No client-facing output unread.** A person reads every message and post, and the client gives a written yes before anything is published.
- **No spending.** Agents never spend money. Clients pay ad budgets directly, and you approve every spend.
- **No unproven claims.** Results, follower counts and phrases like "best in Tunis" need proof, and QA review blocks them.
- **No fake social proof.** Only real reviews and testimonials, with the client's permission.
- **No private client data in chat.** Never paste passwords, ID numbers or payment details. Use role-based access, such as Meta Business Suite, instead.
- **No signing.** Agents draft agreements and invoices. A person signs, and someone qualified checks the agreement template once.
