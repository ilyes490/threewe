# Version recommendation (main reader: university administration)

Three options were compared: (1) the university-facing text, (2) the independent text, (3) a combined text. No option contains a price, and none claims that the university has endorsed anything.

## Option 1: University-facing text (output/mission_services_education.md, unchanged)

**What it says.** A student agency built within the university, proposed for university endorsement, serving campus organizations, SMEs, startups and NGOs with web, social media and events, and training students through supervised real client projects. Section 06 describes three program formats (Semester Internship, Project-Based Collaboration, End-of-Year Capstone), certificates and indicators.

**Strengths.**

- It speaks directly to the administration's interests: training, a certificate, a link between curriculum and industry, a trackable internship host.
- Roles for faculty, mentor and administration are visible.

**Risks for the administration.**

- It asks the university for several things at once: host status, credit for internships, a signatory on the certificate, department participation, PFE recognition. This reads as a request for institutional commitment.
- Wording is hard to test: "a reference student-run agency", "cannot afford a traditional agency", "professional results" have no criterion or source (objectives rows 1, 8, 9).
- It lists NGOs and campus organizations as clients, but the current commercial plan targets startups first and SMEs second. The texts disagree.
- Liability, insurance and the legal entity are not addressed.

**The administration must confirm.** Departments allowed, certificate signatory, internship host status and credits, capstone rules, who carries liability and insurance for students and events.

## Option 2: Independent text (output/agency_identity_independent.md)

**What it says.** A student-built agency for Tunisian startups and small businesses: one named contact, a written brief with objectives, mentor review, a closing report or delivery note, three values, and a vision stated as a measurable aim with numbers still [FILL]. It carries no education program and no university claim.

**Strengths.**

- Every phrase has a definition in the objectives register; the offer catalogue (10 offers, hours and delivery times derived from stated capacity) backs it.
- It asks nothing of the university and shows commercial discipline.

**Risks for the administration.**

- It omits the university link, so the administration cannot see why it should care, what its students gain, or what its name would be attached to.
- It leaves unanswered who the legal entity is, whether students are volunteers, interns or freelancers, and whether the university name may be used. The administration may find the risk hidden rather than managed.
- If the university later learns of the project, it may feel bypassed.

**The administration must confirm.** That the project may exist outside university programs, legal status and liability, whether students may take part, and whether the university name is allowed in any form.

## Option 3: Combined text

**What it says.** The independent core (what is offered, how it is controlled) plus a short, conditional university section: the agency is built within the university and proposed for university endorsement, with training as a stated aim and the requests to the administration listed once, as questions.

**Strengths.** Gives the administration both the substance and its own role; the claims are testable; requests are separated and easy to answer one by one; consistent with the status wording rule.

**Risks for the administration.** It is longer. If the university section grows, it drifts back to Option 1. The administration may answer only the easy requests and leave the liability question open.

**The administration must confirm.** Everything under Options 1 and 2, but as a numbered list of decisions: (a) status of the project, (b) liability and insurance, (c) student participation framework, (d) any certificate or credit, (e) use of the university name.

## Recommendation: Option 3 (combined)

Reasoning:

1. The administration is the decision maker, so it needs both what the agency does (credible, defined) and what is asked of it (explicit, limited).
2. Option 1 asks for the most and proves the least. Option 2 proves the most but gives the administration no role, so it gives no reason to reply.
3. The combined text keeps the independent text's tested wording and moves every university request into one short block of questions, which lowers the commitment the administration perceives.
4. It stays consistent with the rule: "proposed for university endorsement", never an endorsement claim.
