Terms: "brief" = the signed scope document (both words mean the same; "brief" is used). Rows 3, 6 and 21 to 29 apply only to the university version. Capacity basis everywhere: [FILL: weekly hours per producer], working assumption 6 h per week per producer (24 h per week for 4 producers), the same as offer_catalogue.md. Planning basis: 52 weeks - [FILL: 12] pause weeks = 40 teaching weeks; 2 x 6 h x 40 / 12 = 40 h per pool per month (4 producers x 6 h x 40 = 960 h per year). Rows 35 and 36 are superseded by rows 41 and 43 (also marked in the rows). Report wording: projects close with a closing report; single items (Single Design, Small pack) close with a delivery note. Client counts: an active client (row 41) is a point-in-time count over the last 90 days; a completed client (rows 42, 43, 46) is an organization with at least one delivered project, counted cumulatively. The two counts differ by design.

Scope rule: no revenue, price or budget content. Targets are [FILL] where the sources give no justified number. Deadlines marked "proposed" are process deadlines for the founder to confirm. Owners are limited to: Ilyes Chaieb, Taher Bejaoui, Emna Korbi, May Ouesleti, Fayrouz Bouaziz, Badie.
