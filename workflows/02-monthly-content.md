# Workflow 02: Monthly content (start of each month, per client)

1. **Coordinator** reads `clients/[name]/brief.md` and asks the CEO for any missing fact.
2. **Strategy** writes `calendar.md` with the post count of the client's package, themes and dates.
3. **Copywriting** writes one caption per entry in the client's language.
4. **Design brief** writes a one-page brief for each visual.
5. **QA review** checks the whole set, then it goes to `outputs/`.
6. **CEO** approves and moves it to `approved/`. The creative produces the visuals, the client confirms in writing, and a person schedules the posts.
