# Agency Launch Kit: Niche, Packages and Outreach

Nov 9, 2026 · @ilyes

## Your niche: cafés, restaurants and local food businesses

Sell to independent cafés, restaurants and food shops in your city, and to nobody else until you have 3 paying clients there.

- **They live on Instagram and Google Maps.** The need for content never stops and the owner can see its value.
- **Their content repeats** (menu, offers, events), so one template serves many clients.
- **It uses your whole team.** Your creative shoots and edits, your marketing side runs the calendar and ads, your devs sell a digital menu or one-page site.
- **One person decides.** The owner says yes in a meeting or a DM, with no committee.
- **Results are easy to see:** DMs, reservations, calls and Maps views.

Backup niche if food does not work after 30 days: gyms and fitness studios, with the same offer built around membership promotions.

## Three packages

These are starting prices to test with your first prospects, not market data. Adjust them after the first 5 conversations.

| Package | Price (DT/month) | What the client gets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Site Care | 40 | For sites you built: hosting handled, backups, 2 small edits a month, basic SEO check |
| Starter | 250 | 12 designed posts and 8 stories a month, a monthly calendar, profile and Google Maps cleanup, monthly report |
| Growth | 550 | 16 posts, 4 reels, 2 platforms, ads management (ad budget paid by the client), monthly report and a 30-minute call |

**One-off add-on:** a digital menu page with a QR code or a one-page site, 500-800 DT once. It also feeds Site Care, which is your most passive income.

**Rules that protect your time:**

- Pay monthly in advance, no work before payment.
- Posts beyond the package are charged extra.
- Every client gets a 3-month minimum, then monthly.

## Outreach messages

Send these by Instagram DM or WhatsApp. Always fill the bracketed detail with something real and specific from the prospect's page: it is what makes the message get answered.

**Where to find prospects:** Google Maps (cafés and restaurants in your area with few photos, old posts or a weak profile) and Instagram accounts that post less than once a week.

### Message 1: first contact (day 0)

**Arabizi**

Bonjour \[Prénom\], je suis \[Nom\] de \[Agence\]. J'ai regardé la page de \[Café\] et j'ai beaucoup aimé \[détail précis\]. J'ai 3 idées concrètes pour attirer plus de clients grâce à Instagram et Google Maps. Puis-je vous les envoyer ? Sans engagement.

**Français**

Bonjour \[Prénom\], je me permets de revenir vers vous. Voici une des 3 idées : \[exemple concret\]. Je vous envoie les deux autres ?

### Message 3: last follow-up (day 7)

**Arabizi**

Aslema \[Prénom\], akher message menni 😊 Ken ma 3andekch wa9t tawa, mafhoum. Ken t7eb, nal9aw 20 d9i9a el semaine el jeya n7ekiw 3al page mta3ek. Ken le, behi, nconnectiw fil mosta9bal.

**Français**

Bonjour \[Prénom\], dernier message de ma part. Si ce n'est pas le bon moment, aucun souci. Sinon, je vous propose 20 minutes la semaine prochaine pour parler de votre page.

**Rule:** after message 3, stop. Note the prospect as "revisit in 2 months" and move on.

## Weekly routine

Your CEO time is about 2 hours a week. Claude drafts, your team executes, and you approve and sell.

| Day | Who | Task | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | You | Pick 10 new prospects, send message 1 (Claude personalizes the drafts) | 45 min |
| Tuesday | You | Send follow-ups to day-3 and day-7 prospects | 20 min |
| Wednesday | Creative | Shoot or design the week's content from the approved calendar | team time |
| Thursday | Devs | Menu pages, site fixes, one automation or template improvement | team time |
| Friday | You | Approve content, answer clients, book calls with warm prospects | 55 min |
| Last Friday of month | You + Claude | Client reports and renewal check | 30 min |

**Where agents help at zero cost:** prospect lists, DM personalization, content calendars, captions in three languages, report drafts. Your devs can later turn the repeated steps into scripts, but only after a step has worked by hand 5 times.

## 60-day targets and tool budget

The goal is 3 paying clients on the Starter package, which brings 750 DT/month and covers every tool you need with room to spare.

1. **Days 1-7:** put your agency name on the offer sheet and set up one Instagram profile as your portfolio. Do one free sample month for a friendly café so you have real work and a testimonial to show.
2. **Days 8-30:** send message 1 to 10 prospects a week. Aim for 4 calls and 1 paying client.
3. **Days 31-60:** keep the same rhythm and ask every client for a testimonial. Aim for 3 paying clients in total, and offer Site Care to anyone whose site your devs built.

**Assumptions to test, not promises:** about 80 messages in 60 days, roughly 1 in 10 prospects takes a call, roughly 1 in 3 calls becomes a client. If you are far below this after 30 days, change the message or the niche before changing anything else.

**Tool budget rule:** spend 0 DT until the first client pays. After that, tools may take at most 15% of the previous month's revenue, never more than 100 DT. Add one tool at a time, only when a task is costing you more than 2 hours a week by hand.
